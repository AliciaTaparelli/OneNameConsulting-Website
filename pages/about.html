Her story

Over 20 years at ABB, a global technology leader operating in 100+ countries, Lisette held various senior legal, compliance and leadership positions. As Global General Counsel for ABB's Robotics & Discrete Automation Business Area, she led international teams responsible for legal affairs, compliance, investigations, governance, risk management and business integrity. She was a member of ABB Group legal & integrity leadership and a Supervisory Board member.

Throughout her career, she advised executive leadership teams and boards on strategic and operational matters: anti-bribery and corruption compliance, corporate governance, internal investigations, regulatory risk, crisis management, M&A, organizational transformation and culture development. Her experience spans Europe, Asia, Americas and Middle East — giving her deep insight into how compliance and ethical leadership challenges differ across cultures, industries and regulatory environments.

Today, Lisette combines her extensive corporate leadership experience with professional coaching. She's completing an Executive Coaching Program at IMD Business School and pursuing accreditation as a professional coach with the International Coaching Federation. Her coaching approach is grounded in practical leadership experience and focuses on helping leaders navigate complexity and transition, strengthen decision-making, build resilience and create high-performing, ethical organizations.

Her unique combination of executive leadership experience, global compliance expertise and professional coaching enables her to support organizations in building strong cultures of integrity while helping leaders perform at their best in increasingly complex business environments.
