Where and How Do We Start?

We have an initial session to understand your goals and situation and we test our coach-client chemistry. You can bring an overall goal, or choose to explore smaller goals or topics for each session. We have regular one-to-one sessions (online), typically bi-weekly or monthly. You find courage to experiment between sessions and try to find new ways of navigating your challenges. Together we explore and see where you stand and how you feel about your progress. There is no mandatory number of sessions. You decide!

Why Business Coaching?

Leaders reach moments where they need clarity, a sounding board or room to think about their impact. Coaching provides that space: confidential, focused and grounded in the challenges you are actually facing.

Whether working with an individual leader, a leadership team or an organization, my goal is the same: to help people grow, lead with purpose and create lasting impact.
